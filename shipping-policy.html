Shipping Policy

Delivery expectations for orders across Pakistan.

Processing

Most available products are processed within one to two working days. Handmade or personalized items may require additional time.

Delivery time

Standard delivery generally takes three to five working days after dispatch. Remote areas, public holidays and weather may cause delays.

Charges

Delivery charges are confirmed before the order is finalized and may depend on destination, weight and courier.

Tracking

When available, the courier and tracking reference will be shared through WhatsApp or your customer account.

Incorrect address

Customers should verify their phone number and delivery address before confirmation.
