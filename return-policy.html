Event Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand plans can evolve. Here is our transparent policy on event date adjustments, custom prop orders, and deposit refunds.

verified_user

Summary

Date changes requested at least 7 days in advance are accommodated at no extra cost, subject to calendar availability.

chatAsk on WhatsApp

1. Date Modifications & Rescheduling

If your event date or venue changes, please notify us as early as possible. We will transfer your complete advance deposit to the new date provided our stage setup team is available.

2. Cancellations & Advance Deposits

Advance security deposits reserve inventory, raw materials, and manpower exclusively for your slot:

  • 14+ days before event: 80% deposit refund or 100% credit towards a future booking.
  • 7 to 13 days before event: 50% deposit refund to cover procurement and prep costs.
  • Less than 7 days: Advance deposit is non-refundable as fresh florals and custom fabrication begin.

3. Quality Assurance & Setup Handover

Our supervisor will walk you through the completed decor setup before event proceedings commence. Any on-ground lighting adjustments or chair re-alignments will be resolved instantly.
