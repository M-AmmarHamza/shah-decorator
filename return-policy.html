01

When is a product eligible?

Your item must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and returned with its original packaging, labels, accessories, and proof of purchase. The return request must be submitted within seven calendar days of delivery.

02

Items we cannot accept

For hygiene and customization reasons, opened skincare or food products, intimate items, personalized goods, and products marked final sale cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

03

Damaged or incorrect order

Contact us within 48 hours and share clear photos or a short video of the item and packaging. Once verified, we will arrange a replacement or refund without charging return delivery costs.

04

How to request a return

  1. Message PakMarket support on WhatsApp.
  2. Share your name, order details, and reason for returning.
  3. Wait for return confirmation and delivery instructions.
  4. Pack the item safely and send it to the provided address.
05

Refunds and delivery charges

After inspection, approved refunds are processed through the original payment method or bank/mobile-wallet transfer. Please allow 5–10 working days. Original and return delivery charges are non-refundable unless the item was damaged, defective, or incorrect.
