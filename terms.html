Guidelines for event bookings, decor setup, and on-site logistics with Shah Decorator.

Bookings & Date Reservation

An event booking is confirmed upon receipt of the agreed advance deposit and written event detail confirmation via WhatsApp or official channels.

Customization & Materials

Specific floral varieties and customized neon backdrops require advance notice. Seasonal flower substitutions of equal or greater beauty may occur if specific blooms are unavailable.

Venue Access & Power Supply

The client or venue management must provide timely access (typically 4-6 hours prior to event start) and standard electrical power points for fairy lights and stage spotlights.

Payments & Balances

The remaining package balance must be settled on the event day once setup installation is handed over to the client.
